Winter in Port Alder is wet more than it is white, but the roads get salted on frosty mornings and the bay wind does not stop. Here is what we suggest doing before December.

## 1. Protect the paint

A fresh layer of sealant, or a ceramic coating if you have been thinking about one, gives salt and grime something sacrificial to sit on. Coatings need a dry garage night to cure, so book them before the weather turns.

## 2. Coat the glass

A hydrophobic glass coating on the windshield makes rain sheet off at speed and frost easier to scrape. It is one of the most noticeable small upgrades for winter driving.

## 3. Swap to rubber mats

Snow, slush and salt melt into carpet and stay there until spring. Rubber trays keep it where you can tip it out.

## 4. Seal the wheels

Brake dust and road salt together crust onto alloys fast in winter. A wheel sealant makes them far easier to rinse clean.

## 5. Keep rinsing

The most useful winter habit is the simplest: a gentle rinse every week or two, underneath as well as on top, to stop salt sitting on the car.
