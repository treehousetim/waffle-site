This is a draft: the dates and the list are not final yet, so it is shown only in development builds.

## What we are planning

Every December the counter changes. Here is what we are planning to bake this year, and how to make sure you get some.

- **Stollen**, made with our own candied peel and a rope of marzipan, from the first week of December.
- **Panettone**, naturally leavened over three days, in two sizes.
- **Pies** for the holidays: apple and cider, pumpkin, and a pecan pie that contains a great many nuts.
- **Party trays** of mini croissants, savory buns and cardamom knots, for twelve or twenty-four.
- **Whole cakes** from the celebration menu, including brown butter and pear.

## Ordering

Orders open in mid-November, through the [cake order form](/cakes/order/) or at the counter. The last day to order will be about a week before the holidays, and the busiest pickup days book up fast.

## December hours

We will post them here. We expect to close for a few days after the holidays, to rest the ovens and ourselves, much as we do [every Monday](/journal/why-we-close-on-mondays/).
