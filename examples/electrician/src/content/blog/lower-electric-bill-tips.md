Roughly in order of how fast they pay for themselves:

1. **Swap the last incandescent and halogen bulbs** for LEDs.
2. **Kill standby loads** with switched outlets behind the TV and desk.
3. **Put outdoor lights on motion sensors or timers.**
4. **Use the utility's off-peak rate** for EV charging and laundry, if it offers one.
5. **Install a smart thermostat**, wired with a C-wire so it does not drain its battery.
6. **Seal and insulate around recessed cans**, or replace old cans with sealed LED wafers.
7. **Replace an old electric water heater** with a heat-pump model.
8. **Add ceiling fans** so the AC can be set a few degrees higher.
9. **Fix flickering and buzzing**, which often signals wasted power at a bad connection.
10. **Consider a heat pump** when the furnace or AC is due for replacement.
