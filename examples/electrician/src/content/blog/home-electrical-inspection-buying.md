General home inspectors are generalists. They open the panel, try some outlets and note anything obvious. An electrician tests.

## What a separate inspection finds

- **Double-tapped breakers**, two wires under a screw made for one.
- **Breakers too big for their wire**, which can let a circuit overheat.
- **Ungrounded three-prong outlets**, a sign someone swapped the outlets and not the wiring.
- **Aluminum branch wiring** or remaining **knob-and-tube**.
- **DIY work** in the attic, basement or garage.

## Why it pays for itself

A report with a photo and a price for every item is a strong negotiating position: you can ask for repairs, a credit or a lower price. And you know what to budget for in the first year.
