Every December we get calls about holiday lights that trip the breaker. Most of them come down to water and load.

## Keep connections dry

GFCI outlets trip when current leaks to ground, and wet plugs leak. Lift connections off the ground, use covered outdoor connectors, and point plugs downward so water runs off.

## Mind the load

LED strings draw very little, but inflatables and older incandescent strings add up. Spread them across more than one outdoor circuit if you can.

## Use outdoor-rated cords and a timer

Indoor extension cords crack in the cold. A timer saves power and saves you from forgetting.

## A dedicated outdoor circuit

If you decorate every year, a weatherproof outdoor outlet on its own circuit by the porch or garage ends the extension-cord tangle for good. [Ask us about one](/contact/).
