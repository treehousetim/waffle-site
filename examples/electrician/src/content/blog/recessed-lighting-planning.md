The most common recessed lighting mistake is the runway: a perfect grid across the ceiling that lights the floor evenly and the room badly.

## A starting rule

Divide the ceiling height by two for the spacing between lights. An 8-foot ceiling gives roughly 4 feet between fixtures, and about half that from the walls.

## Light what you look at

- **Kitchens:** put the lights over the front edge of the counters, so you are not working in your own shadow.
- **Living rooms:** wash the walls and the art; add lamps for the sofa.
- **Hallways:** one light every 8 to 10 feet is plenty.

## Wafer lights

Slim LED wafer lights sit in a hole the size of the fixture, with no can above. They are ideal for retrofits under insulated ceilings and low basement ceilings.
