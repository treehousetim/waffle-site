If it has been years since you last saw a dentist, the hardest part is booking the appointment. Once you are here, you will find the first visit back is calm, private and entirely without judgement. We see people after long gaps every single week, and we are always glad they came.

## Why people stay away

Almost everyone who comes back after a long gap has a reason, and they are all ordinary: a bad experience as a child, fear of needles or the noise, a busy decade, money worries, moving town, or simply feeling that it has been too long to go back now. That last one is the most common, and it is the one I most want to talk you out of.

We are not keeping score. Nobody here will tell you off, sigh at your x-rays, or make you feel small. If you feel embarrassed, please know that whatever is going on in your mouth, we have seen it many times before.

## What the first visit back is like

1. **A conversation first, sitting up.** I ask what has been happening, what is bothering you, if anything, and how you feel about being here. If you are nervous, we can do this part in a normal chair with a cup of tea.
2. **A gentle look.** I look at your teeth, gums and the rest of your mouth, and explain what I see as I go, or afterwards if you prefer.
3. **X-rays, if needed.** After a long gap, a few x-rays help us see between teeth and under the gums. I will always say why before taking one.
4. **Your plan, without pressure.** I show you what I found on a screen and talk you through what is urgent, what can wait, and what is optional. You take it home to think about.

Many people find they need less work than they feared. Some need more, and that is fine too: we can spread treatment over months, at a pace you can manage, with the most important things first.

## You do not have to do everything at once

A long-gap visit is the start of a conversation, not a list of demands. If the idea of a cleaning straight away is too much, we can book it for another day. If money is a worry, tell us early: we will give you prices before anything happens, and our [insurance and payment](/insurance/) page explains our monthly plan and membership.

## If you are anxious

Tell us when you book. We will give you a longer slot, Jo will call you first to say hello, and we will agree a stop signal before we start. Raise your hand, and we stop. Every time. There is more on our [nervous patients](/nervous-patients/) page, and some practical tips in [small things that help with dental nerves](/blog/small-things-for-dental-anxiety/).

## One small step

You do not need to feel ready. You only need to make one phone call, or fill in one short form. We will take it from there, slowly.
