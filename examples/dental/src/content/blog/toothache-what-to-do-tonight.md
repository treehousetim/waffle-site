If a tooth is aching tonight, take an over-the-counter painkiller as directed on the packet, rinse with warm salty water, keep your head raised when you lie down, and call a dentist as soon as they open. If your face is swelling, you have a fever, or you are finding it hard to breathe or swallow, do not wait for the morning: go to an emergency room.

That is the short version. Here is the longer one, for the long night.

## Things that help

- **Pain relief, as the packet says.** Follow the dose on the label, and check with a pharmacist if you take other medicines or have health conditions. Never put a tablet against the gum next to the tooth: it can burn the gum and does nothing for the pain.
- **Warm salty water.** Half a teaspoon of salt in a glass of warm water, swished gently and spat out, can soothe sore gums and clear trapped food.
- **Floss, gently.** A surprising number of “toothaches” are a piece of popcorn husk or seed wedged between teeth. Floss carefully around the sore tooth.
- **A cold pack on your cheek.** Twenty minutes on, twenty off, if there is swelling or the pain throbs.
- **Sleep propped up.** An extra pillow can make a throbbing tooth feel less angry.
- **Chew on the other side,** and avoid very hot, very cold or sugary food and drink.

## Things that do not help

Heat packs on a swollen face, aspirin on the gum, and “waiting to see if it goes away” for weeks. Pain that fades on its own is not always good news: a tooth can stop hurting because the nerve inside has died, while an infection carries on quietly.

## When not to wait for the morning

Go to an emergency room or call 911 if you have:

- swelling in your face or neck that is spreading, or affecting your eye;
- difficulty breathing or swallowing;
- a high temperature with a swollen face;
- an injury to your face or jaw, or a head injury.

Those need hospital care first. We will see you afterwards for the tooth.

## Knocked-out tooth?

For an adult tooth that has been knocked out, time matters. Pick it up by the white crown, not the root. If it is dirty, rinse it briefly in milk. If you can, push it gently back into its socket and bite on a clean cloth; if not, keep it in milk or tucked inside your cheek, and call us straight away. Baby teeth should not be put back in, but do call us.

## What to tell us when you call

We keep same-day appointments free every day we are open. When you call, it helps us to know:

- which tooth, roughly, and how long it has hurt;
- whether the pain is constant or comes and goes, and what sets it off: cold, hot, biting;
- whether there is swelling, and where;
- any medicines you have taken, and when.

You do not need to be a Brightside patient. If you are in pain, we will see you.

## What happens at the appointment

We find the cause first, usually with a look and a small x-ray, and make you comfortable. Sometimes we can fix the problem there and then; sometimes we do a temporary repair and plan the rest for another day, once things have calmed down. We always explain before we start, and you decide what happens next.
