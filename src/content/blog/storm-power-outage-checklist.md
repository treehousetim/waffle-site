## Before the storm

- Charge phones and battery packs.
- Know where your main breaker is, and make sure the panel is not blocked by boxes.
- Test your sump pump and its battery backup.
- If you have a generator, run it for ten minutes and check the fuel.

## During the outage

- Unplug computers, TVs and anything with a motor, to protect them from the surge when power returns.
- Leave one light switched on so you know when the power is back.
- Run portable generators outdoors only, at least 20 feet from doors and windows.

## After the storm

- Stay away from downed lines, and anything touching them, including fences and puddles.
- If your **service drop** (the line to your house) is pulled down or the meter is damaged, the utility will not reconnect until an electrician repairs the mast or meter base. [Call our emergency line](/emergency/).
- If water reached outlets, the panel or appliances, do not switch them back on until they have been checked.
