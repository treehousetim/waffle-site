A regular switch is just a break in the hot wire. A smart switch is a small computer, and it needs power even when the light is off. Most get it from the **neutral** wire.

## Do you have one?

Switch off the breaker, take the switch plate off and look in the box. A bundle of white wires twisted together under a wire nut, not connected to the switch, is the neutral. Homes wired since the mid-1980s usually have one in every switch box.

## If there is no neutral

- **No-neutral smart switches** exist, though some do not work well with low-wattage LEDs.
- **Run a neutral** to the box: often simple from an attic or basement.
- **Smart bulbs** avoid the problem, but the wall switch must stay on.
