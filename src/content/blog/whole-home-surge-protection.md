Power strips with surge protection guard what is plugged into them. Your furnace, range, dishwasher and HVAC controls are not plugged into one.

## How it works

A whole-home surge protective device (SPD) mounts at the panel and diverts voltage spikes to ground before they travel through your circuits. The 2020 electrical code requires one when a service is replaced or upgraded.

## What it protects

Appliances and electronics with circuit boards, which today means almost everything: furnaces, heat pumps, washers, garage door openers, smart switches.

## What it does not

A direct lightning strike to the house is beyond any protector. Keep point-of-use protection on sensitive electronics, as a second layer.

## Worth it?

For the price of one appliance repair, it protects all of them. We often fit one during a [panel upgrade](/services/panel-upgrades/), when it costs least.
