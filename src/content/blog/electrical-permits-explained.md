A permit is not paperwork for its own sake. It means an inspector who does not work for the electrician checks the work before the walls close.

## What usually needs one

- Panel replacements and service upgrades
- New circuits, including for EV chargers, hot tubs and generators
- Rewiring
- Basement finishing and additions

Swapping a light fixture or an outlet like-for-like usually does not.

## Why it matters to you

- **Insurance:** a fire traced to unpermitted work can complicate a claim.
- **Selling:** buyers' inspectors and lenders increasingly ask.
- **Safety:** a second set of eyes on work you will never see again.
