When a second electric car arrives, the instinct is to double everything. Usually you do not need to.

## Load sharing

Many chargers can be paired so they share one circuit. When both cars are plugged in, each gets half the power; when one finishes, the other gets it all. Overnight, both cars are full by morning.

## Scheduling

Staggered charging windows in each car's app do the same job with no extra hardware, if both drivers remember.

## When a bigger circuit makes sense

Two long commutes and short nights can justify more capacity. A load calculation tells us whether your panel has room.
