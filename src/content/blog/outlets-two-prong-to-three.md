It is tempting to buy a pack of three-prong outlets and swap them in. Without a ground wire behind them, that creates outlets that look grounded and are not.

## Three proper options

1. **Run a ground.** If the circuit has a path back to the panel, a ground wire can sometimes be added without rewiring.
2. **Use GFCI protection.** Code allows a three-prong outlet on an ungrounded circuit if it is GFCI-protected and labelled "No equipment ground". It protects people, though not sensitive electronics.
3. **Rewire the circuit.** The complete answer, and the right one where the old wiring is also tired.

## Test what you have

An inexpensive plug-in tester shows "open ground" on an ungrounded three-prong outlet. If you find several, it is worth a [proper look](/services/inspections/).
