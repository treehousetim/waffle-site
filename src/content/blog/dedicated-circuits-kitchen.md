Kitchens use more power than any other room, in short bursts, all at once.

## What usually gets its own circuit

- **Refrigerator**, so a tripped countertop breaker does not spoil the food.
- **Dishwasher** and **garbage disposal**, sometimes shared where allowed.
- **Built-in microwave**.
- **Electric range or cooktop**, on a 240-volt circuit.

## The countertops

Current code calls for at least two 20-amp small-appliance circuits for the countertop outlets, all GFCI-protected, with outlets spaced so no point along the counter is more than two feet from one.

## Remodels are the moment

With the walls open, new circuits cost a fraction of what they cost later. Ask your electrician to plan the circuits with the layout, not after it.
