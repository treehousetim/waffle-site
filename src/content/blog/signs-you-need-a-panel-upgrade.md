Your panel is where every circuit in the house begins. When it is too small or too old, the symptoms show up everywhere else.

1. **The service is 100 amps or less.** Check the main breaker at the top of the panel. A modern home with central air usually wants 200.
2. **There are no free spaces.** Tandem breakers squeezed in to make room are a sign the panel is full.
3. **Lights dim when the AC or dryer starts.** Some dimming is normal; a lot is not.
4. **Breakers trip often**, especially in rooms that were added on later.
5. **You still have fuses.** Fuse boxes work, but most insurers and lenders now ask about them.
6. **The panel is a brand with a history of failures.** Some older panels have breakers that may not trip under overload.
7. **You are planning a heat pump, an EV charger or a kitchen remodel.** Each one adds a large load.
