A burning-plastic or oddly fishy smell near an outlet, switch or panel is one of the few electrical symptoms that should never wait until morning.

## Right now

1. If you see smoke or flames, leave and call 911.
2. Unplug anything plugged into that outlet, if it is safe to touch.
3. Switch off the circuit at the panel. If you cannot tell which one, switch off the main.
4. Do not use that outlet again until it has been checked.
5. [Call our 24/7 line](/emergency/).

## What usually causes it

- A loose connection heating up behind the outlet, often from "backstabbed" wires.
- An overloaded outlet: a space heater on a power strip is the classic.
- A failing device or appliance cord.

Even when the smell fades, the damage behind the plate does not repair itself. It needs opening up and checking.
