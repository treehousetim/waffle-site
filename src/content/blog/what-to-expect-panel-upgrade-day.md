A panel upgrade is a big job, but on the day it follows a predictable rhythm.

## The morning

The utility disconnects the service, usually first thing. From that point the house has no power. We remove the old panel, label every circuit, and mount the new one.

## The middle of the day

Each circuit moves to the new panel, on a correctly sized breaker. We fit grounding and bonding to current code, plus AFCI and GFCI protection where required, and often whole-home surge protection.

## The afternoon

The inspector checks the work. Once it passes, the utility reconnects and we test every circuit with you.

## How to prepare

- Work from somewhere else for the day if you can.
- Empty what you can from the freezer, or keep it closed.
- Clear a path to the panel and the meter.
