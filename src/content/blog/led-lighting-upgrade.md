Switching to LED is the easiest energy saving in a house. Most of the problems people have with it come from what the LEDs are connected to.

## Flicker on dimmers

Old dimmers were built for incandescent bulbs, which draw far more power. Put a few LEDs on one and it cannot regulate properly: you get flicker, a narrow dimming range, or lights that never quite go off. The fix is an **LED-rated dimmer** matched to the fixtures, and dimmable bulbs.

## Buzzing

Buzz usually comes from the dimmer or from a cheap LED driver. A quality dimmer and fixtures from the same family solve most of it.

## Color temperature

- **2700K** is warm, like old incandescent bulbs, and suits living rooms and bedrooms.
- **3000K** is a touch crisper, good for kitchens.
- **4000K** and above feels like an office.

Pick one temperature per room. Mixing them looks wrong even when people cannot say why.
