Where the flicker happens tells you most of what you need to know.

## One fixture

Usually the bulb, a loose bulb, or an LED on a dimmer not rated for it. Try a different bulb first.

## One room or circuit

A loose connection at a switch, an outlet or a splice somewhere on that circuit. Loose connections create heat, so this is worth fixing soon.

## When the AC, furnace or dryer starts

A brief dip is normal. A deep or long one suggests the circuit is overloaded or the service is undersized.

## The whole house, at random

This is the one to take seriously: a loose or corroded connection at the service, meter or main breaker, sometimes on the utility's side. Call the utility and an electrician.
