Heat pumps are efficient, but they are big electrical loads, especially with backup electric heat strips.

## What a heat pump needs

- A **dedicated 240-volt circuit**, often 30 to 60 amps for the outdoor unit.
- Another circuit for the air handler and any backup heat strips, which can be large.
- A disconnect within sight of the outdoor unit.

## Will the panel cope?

A load calculation answers it. Many 200-amp panels manage easily. A 100-amp panel with an electric range and dryer often does not, and that is when a [panel upgrade](/services/panel-upgrades/) enters the conversation.

## Avoiding the upgrade

A properly sized system with smaller heat strips, or a load manager that pauses the water heater while the heat strips run, can keep you within your existing service. Talk to your electrician before the HVAC quote is final.
